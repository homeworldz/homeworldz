# Default Terrain Texture License

Two sets live here: the unmodified Second Life Viewer artwork, retained as the
provenance of the second set, and the 1024x1024 adaptations of it that regions
actually use.

## The adaptations regions use (CC BY-SA 3.0)

- `98324081-ab9d-49a7-8406-f70e83d813d7.png` — layer 1, dirt
- `cf2a4b28-cf6d-44ce-8e70-1ea4cb616a6d.png` — layer 2, grass
- `26700e50-492d-4243-9513-1905e8109e2b.png` — layer 3, mountain
- `9a2248ec-28de-40a0-a459-9873e1f7464c.png` — layer 4, rock

**These are modified works.** Each was produced from the corresponding
unmodified file below by generative AI upscaling (ChatGPT) to a larger,
seamlessly tiling image, then resampled from 1254x1254 to 1024x1024 with a
wrapping filter. The upscaling invents detail that is not present in the
source, so these are adaptations rather than reproductions.

Because the source is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0, these adaptations are licensed under the same
terms, and the attribution below applies to them as it does to the originals.

Wrap continuity was measured rather than assumed: each tile's wrap edge differs
from its neighbouring column by 0.98 to 1.76 times a typical interior step
(1.0 being a perfect tile), and resampling to 1024 left those figures
unchanged. Layer 4 is the least continuous of the four.

## The unmodified originals (CC BY-SA 3.0)

Retained so the provenance of the adaptations above is verifiable rather than
asserted. No region references these ids.

- `b8d3965a-ad78-bf43-699b-bff8eca6c975.j2c` (`Terrain Dirt`)
- `abb783e6-3e93-26c0-248a-247666855da3.j2c` (`Terrain Grass`)
- `179cdabd-398a-9b6b-1391-4dc333ba321f.j2c` (`Terrain Mountain`)
- `beb169c7-11ea-fff2-efe5-0f24dc881df2.j2c` (`Terrain Rock`)

These four are unmodified Second Life Viewer artwork from the Halcyon
`TexturesAssetSet`, 128x128 each. Homeworldz renamed the source files to their
existing asset UUIDs but did not change their contents.
